We do not use your data for: advertising, profiling, machine learning model training, resale to data brokers, or any purpose not listed above.

8 Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously and rely on industry-standard practices provided by Supabase's infrastructure:

  • Encryption in transit — all data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher.
  • Encryption at rest — data stored in the Supabase database is encrypted at rest using AES-256.
  • Password security — passwords are never stored in plain text. Supabase Auth uses bcrypt hashing with a work factor designed to resist brute-force attacks.
  • Row-level security (RLS) — database access policies ensure that each user can only access their own data, even at the infrastructure level.
  • Access controls — only the app's service role key, stored securely server-side, can access the database. Your client receives only a limited anonymous key.

Despite these measures, no security system is impenetrable. We cannot guarantee the absolute security of your data. If you discover a vulnerability or security concern, please disclose it responsibly by emailing us directly before making it public.

11 California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), grants you specific rights regarding your personal information.

Categories of personal information collected

In the past 12 months, we have collected the following categories of personal information as defined by the CCPA:

  • Identifiers — email address
  • Internet or other electronic network activity — IP address, session timestamps
  • Other personal information you voluntarily provide — climbing project names, notes, attempt records

Your CCPA rights

  • Right to Know — you may request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you.
  • Right to Delete — you may request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to Correct — you may request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale or Sharing — we do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. No opt-out is necessary.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination — we will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA rights.

To submit a CCPA request, email us at projecta.climbing.app@gmail.com. We will verify your identity before processing the request and respond within 45 days (extendable by an additional 45 days when reasonably necessary).

13 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

The Projecta mobile app does not use browser cookies. However, our infrastructure provider Supabase uses session tokens to maintain your authenticated state within the app. These tokens are stored securely in the device's local storage and are used solely for authentication purposes.

We do not use:

  • Advertising cookies or tracking pixels
  • Cross-app or cross-site tracking technologies
  • Fingerprinting or device identifiers for analytics
  • Third-party analytics SDKs (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude)

15 Account Deletion

You may request the deletion of your Projecta account and all associated data at any time. To do so:

  1. Send an email to projecta.climbing.app@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
  2. Include the email address associated with your account.
  3. We will verify your identity and confirm the deletion request.
  4. Your account and all personal data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of confirmation.

Please note that once deleted, your data cannot be recovered. Server-side infrastructure logs managed by Supabase may persist for up to 90 days after deletion, after which they are automatically purged.
